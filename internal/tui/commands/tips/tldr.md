# /tldr

A terse executive digest of the session: what was asked, what changed, what
remains — a few lines, model-written.

## Usage
- `/tldr` — the digest.

## Notes
- Shorter and more opinionated than `/summary`.
- Costs one small completion; instant alternatives are `/recap`.

## Related
- `/summary` — the fuller narrative.
- `/recap` — the deterministic digest.
