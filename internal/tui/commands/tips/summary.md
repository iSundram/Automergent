# /summary

Asks the model to write a concise summary of the conversation so far — the
arc of what was asked, decided and changed — and shows it in the transcript.

## When to use
- Handing off to a colleague or a future session.
- Before `/compact` or `/new` when you want the essence kept in view.

## Notes
- The summary is generated by the active model; it costs one completion.
- It is added to the conversation, not persisted as a file (use `/export`
  for a file).

## Related
- `/recap` — the deterministic digest (no model call, instant).
- `/export` — the full raw transcript as Markdown.
