# /collapse

Folds every collapsible conversation block to its header line: tool cards
to their name and summary, thinking blocks to "✓ Thought for Ns", shell
output to the command row and a one-line tail. Long sessions get their
vertical space back.

## Notes
- The status line names the inverse (`/expand`) after it runs.
- Collapsed blocks keep their content — expanding restores it exactly.

## Related
- `/expand` — the inverse.
- `/compact` — shrink the model's context, not the display.
