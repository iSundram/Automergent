# /feedback

Opens the feedback flow: a short structured report (what happened, what you
expected) sent to the project's feedback channel.

## Usage
- `/feedback <text>` — send inline feedback.

## Notes
- Nothing about your session content is attached unless you include it.
- The report includes the version and platform for reproducibility.

## Related
- `/doctor` — attach diagnostics yourself when reporting a bug.
